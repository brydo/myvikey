Our Story

V I Key® was created to bring physical items and digital spaces together in a meaningful way. We believe that technology should be simple, helpful, and serve a real human purpose—whether that means making everyday tasks easier or holding onto the memories that matter most.

Using simple tap-and-go NFC technology, we turn physical items into instant keys that open digital pages. Every piece we make is crafted with care, respect, and a strong commitment to privacy, giving you a smooth and secure way to share information.

The Key to What Matters Most

The connections we make in business, life, and family are the most important parts of our world. V I Key® serves as a physical bridge to those connections. It is a tangible item you can hold in your hand—a key that unlocks a digital space, preserves important stories, and opens the door to the moments you want to keep close.

Why We Started

We started V I Key® from a deeply personal place after losing someone who meant everything to us. We searched for a dignified, private way to keep their memory alive, but found that public social media feeds, impersonal websites, and fleeting posts quickly got lost in the online noise.

We wanted something different: a quiet, beautiful space that belonged only to the people who shared the love.

As we built this private space, we realized this simple, beautiful way of connecting physical objects to digital links could help people in other areas of life, too. It can replace paper business cards that get thrown away or messy barcodes that ruin a product's look. Born from loss, built with love, and expanded to help with everyday life, V I Key® is a gentle, modern way to stay connected.
