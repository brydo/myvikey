Our Story

Our Purpose

V I Key® was created to bring physical items and digital spaces together in a meaningful way. We believe that technology should be simple, helpful, and serve a real human need — not add complexity. Our physical keys use NFC to create instant, private connections to carefully crafted web pages that put memories, information, and contact details exactly where they're needed.

By using simple tap-and-go NFC technology, we create physical keys that open digital pages instantly. Every piece we make is crafted with care, respect, and a commitment to privacy. Our memorial pages are private by default and can be shared only with those who need them.

🗝️

The Key to What Matters Most

The connections we make in business, life, and family are the most important parts of our world. V I Key® serves as a physical bridge to those connections. It is a small, dignified object that opens a private space where stories, photos, and important information can be stored and accessed easily.

Why We Started

We started V I Key® from a deeply personal place after losing someone who meant everything to us. We searched for a dignified, private way to keep their memory alive and share it with the people who mattered most. The result was a quiet, respectful digital space accessible by a simple tap.

We wanted something different — something that felt personal, private, and beautiful. As we developed this, we realised the same idea could help businesses and families in many other ways: smart menu links for restaurants, business profiles, Wi‑Fi sharing, contact cards, and more.
