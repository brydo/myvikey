The Key to What Matters Most

V I Key stands for Very Important Key.

Because the people and pets we love are the most important parts of our lives. And the memories we hold of them are the key that keeps them close — a key that unlocks love, preserves stories, and opens the door to moments that never truly fade.

 

 

Why We Started  V I key® Memorials

We started V I key® Memorials from a deeply personal place — after losing someone who meant the world to us. We searched for a way to keep their memory alive that felt dignified, private, and truly personal. What we found were public social media pages, impersonal memorial sites, or fleeting posts that soon got lost in the noise.

We wanted something different. A quiet, beautiful space that belonged only to the people who shared the love. A place where photos and stories could live safely — not for the world, but for the ones who mattered most.

That longing became V I key®. Born from loss, built with love, and offered to anyone who needs a gentle way to hold on.

What We Do

We create private memorial pages that open through a simple link or NFC tag. Each page is secure, personal, and yours for as long as you want it.

You can choose to protect your page with a password. Only the people you share the link and password with — family, friends, or loved ones — will be able to view the tribute. This ensures your memories remain private, respected, and seen only by those you trust.

Your memorial page can be shared with anyone you choose. A link and a password are all they need to access the tribute, no matter where they are.

NFC Memory Tags

NFC Memory Tags are real, lasting keepsakes — not just a QR code printed on paper or a screenshot that can be copied or lost. Each NFC tag is a physical item you can hold, place, or carry, and with a simple tap it opens your private memorial page. It's a safer, more personal, and more meaningful way to keep memories close.

Your memorial link can be placed on:
• NFC fobs
• Small NFC plaques
• Home items or keepsakes

A simple tap with any phone opens the private memorial page instantly — a modern, heartfelt way to keep memories close and share them with the people who matter most.
