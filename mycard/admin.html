🔑 Card Password (optional)

Uses the Page ID entered above. Cards without a password are public.

👤 Owner Access

Leave the Page ID blank to create a brand-new card (an ID is generated), or enter an existing one to issue it a new code. Owners claim the card at /mycard/owner with the code, then add their own details. Codes never expire, are shown only once and a new code replaces the old one.

📦 Batch of Cards

Create several blank cards at once for printing (code under a scratch panel). The list is emailed to you and can be downloaded. It is the only time the codes are shown, so keep it private.

🤝 Subcontractors

Give a subcontractor a card allowance. You then allocate cards to them (codes and URLs are emailed to you only). They sign in at /mycard/sub and see a read-only list: which of their cards are claimed and the name on each. They never see URLs, codes or contact details. Only you can set their logo: it then shows on all their cards and their card owners cannot change it. Switch them off at any time.
