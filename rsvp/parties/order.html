How It Works

Three simple steps from your design to your guests' doorsteps.

1

You Design & Print

Create your party invitations your way — your colours, your wording, your style. Print them with your usual printer.

2

Send Them to Us

Post your printed cards to us. We apply and programme a discreet NFC tag on each card, linking it to your personalised RSVP page.

3

We Return Them

Your tagged cards come back ready to send. Guests tap their phone on the card and your RSVP page opens instantly.
