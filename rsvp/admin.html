Edit page wording

Card text & surface

Solid card surfaces keep text readable over any background. Heading and body colours must each meet 4.5:1 contrast against the surface, button labels 4.5:1 against the button background, and the button & field border 3:1 against the surface. Button hover and focus states are derived automatically so they stay readable. The preview shows your exact colours; combinations that are too hard to read are explained next to the colour and cannot be saved. Sizes and spacing are bounded.
