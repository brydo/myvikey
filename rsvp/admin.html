Category copy & card style

These settings affect the event theme selected above and can change its landing page, invitation copy and card style. Invitation titles, messages and replies stay separate.

Use a trusted, publicly accessible image, without passwords or private access tokens. Leave blank for the category’s colour background.

Edit page wording

Plain text only. Blank text hides optional wording. Keep labels meaningful so guests can understand the form. These display settings are public; do not include private information.

Card text & surface

Solid card surfaces keep text readable over any background. Heading and body colours must each meet 4.5:1 contrast against the surface, button labels 4.5:1 against the button background, and the button & field border 3:1 against the surface. Button hover and focus states are derived automatically so they stay readable. The preview shows your exact colours; combinations that are too hard to read are explained next to the colour and cannot be saved. Sizes and spacing are bounded.
