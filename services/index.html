Everyday Uses & Solutions

  • Digital Business Cards: Share your contact details, phone number, and email instantly. People can save your info straight to their phone contacts with a single tap.
  • All-in-One Social Links: Bring all your social media platforms—like Instagram, LinkedIn, Facebook, or YouTube—together onto one tidy, unified page.
  • Direct Website Routing: Send people straight to your company website, online store, or portfolio without them having to type a long web address.
  • Easy Feedback & Reviews: Place a tag on a counter or product so customers can tap to leave a Google or Trustpilot review instantly.
  • Instant Wi-Fi Access: Let guests connect to your home or office Wi-Fi automatically, without having to type out long, confusing passwords.
  • Digital Menus & Price Lists: Great for small businesses and cafes. Update your menu or prices online anytime without needing to print new paper copies.

Memory & Legacy Tags

Alongside our everyday business uses, we remain deeply dedicated to our original mission: offering a private space to honour lost loved ones, close friends, and cherished pets.

  • Private Tribute Spaces: A quiet, respectful page to hold photos, voice notes, stories, and moments that deserve to be remembered, away from public comments and noise.
  • Complete Privacy Control: You can password-protect your memory page. Only the family and friends you share the password with will ever be able to see it.
  • Physical Keepsakes: Our memory tags are lasting, tangible items like key fobs, small plaques, or home keepsakes. A simple tap with a phone brings their memory right to your screen—a heartfelt way to keep stories alive across generations.
