Lost & Found

A little tag to help bring a much-loved pet or treasured item back home.

This service is being prepared and is not yet available to order.

How it works

  1. Claim your tag

    Use your claim code to add details and choose how a finder can contact you.

  2. A finder taps the tag

    A compatible phone opens the tag's page, where the finder can see the details you chose to share.

  3. Get in touch

    After a bot check, the finder can use the call, WhatsApp or text buttons you enabled. They can choose to include their location in a message.

You're in control

Use your private manage link to update details, choose when contact buttons are available, or mark your tag lost. Lost mode displays an alert and switches off automatically after 14 days unless renewed.

Keep your manage link safe: anyone with it can edit or delete the tag, and it cannot be recovered if lost.

Try the demo

Explore owner setup, finder view and lost mode using a sample pet. The demo keeps its details in your browser and does not activate a real tag.
