General V I Key services

This section explains how information is handled across the V I Key website and services, except for Santa Vi-Key orders, which are covered separately below. The information involved depends on the service you use.

Information you give us

You may provide contact and order details, profile or memorial content, event information, RSVP responses, or details for a Lost & Found tag. Only provide information needed for the service. Avoid putting sensitive personal information or someone else's private details in free-text fields.

How information is used and who can see it

We use information to provide and manage the service you request, display content you choose to publish, answer enquiries, fulfil orders, and maintain or protect our services.

Content on a shareable card, memorial, event page, or Lost & Found tag page may be seen by people who open that page. Contact and sharing options vary by service. Before adding details or a photo, consider who may see it and include only what is needed. For a Lost & Found tag, do not add a home address, exact location, sensitive personal details, or photos that show people's faces or identifying information.

Some cards, memorials and event pages use separately hosted images. A public image link may be opened directly, even if the page itself has a password. Image files can contain hidden information such as location; remove private metadata before uploading.

Who helps us provide the service

We use online hosting and storage providers to run our services and email providers to handle service messages. When needed to fulfil an order, relevant details may also be shared with payment or delivery providers. These providers handle information under their own privacy terms.

How long information is kept

How long we keep information depends on the service. Published profile, memorial, or event content may remain available until its owner or administrator removes it. Order and service records may be kept for as long as needed to provide the service and meet legal or accounting requirements.

Removing a page or changing an image link does not automatically delete a separately hosted image. The hosted file may need to be removed separately.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us to provide, correct, or delete personal information, subject to applicable legal requirements. To make a request or ask a question, email vikeyshop@outlook.com. If you are unhappy with how your information has been handled, you can contact the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Santa Vi-Key

This section applies to Santa Vi-Key orders and personalised Santa messages. For other V I Key services, see the general section above.

What information is collected

A parent or guardian may provide a child's first name, town, and details for the personalised message, such as achievements or gifts. We also collect the contact and delivery details needed to fulfil the order, such as the purchaser's name, email address, and delivery address.

How information is used

We use the child's details to create the personalised message and set up the Santa Vi-Key. Order details are stored in our online order system and sent to us by email so we can process and fulfil the order.

Payment is handled through NatWest Payit. We do not receive or store your bank login or card details. We share only the information needed with payment and delivery providers to complete your order.

We do not use this information for marketing or advertising, and we do not sell it.

How long Santa information is kept

Personalised messages, pages, links, and other child-specific details are deleted by 31 January each year (31 January 2027 for the 2026 Christmas season). New Santa submission and letter KV records expire automatically on that date. Existing records created before this expiry control was added are not backdated; they must be deleted separately. Basic order records that do not include children's details may be kept for as long as required for accounting.

Next Christmas

Questions or requests

You can ask us to see, correct, or delete your or your child's information before the scheduled deletion date. Email vikeyshop@outlook.com.
