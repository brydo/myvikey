General V I Key services

This section covers the V I Key website and services other than Santa Vi-Key. The Santa-specific details are in the separate section below.

Information you provide

Depending on the service, you may provide contact or order details, profile or memorial content, event information, or RSVP responses. Only provide information needed for the service, and avoid adding sensitive personal information to free-text fields.

How information is used and shown

We use the information you provide to set up and operate the service you request, display content you choose to publish, respond to enquiries, process orders, and maintain or protect the service. Content on a shareable card, memorial, or event page may be visible to people who have its link; use any available password or sharing controls for content you want to restrict.

Service providers and storage

V I Key uses online hosting and storage providers to run its services and email providers to handle service messages. Where needed to fulfil an order, relevant details may also be shared with payment or delivery providers. Those providers handle information under their own privacy terms.

How long information is kept

Retention depends on the service. Published profile, memorial, or event content may remain available until it is removed by its owner or administrator. Order and service records may be kept while needed to provide the service and meet legal or accounting requirements. To ask about, correct, or request deletion of information, email vikeyshop@outlook.com.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law, you can ask us to provide, correct, or delete personal information, subject to applicable legal requirements. If you are unhappy with how information has been handled, you can contact the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Santa Vi-Key

This section applies specifically to Santa Vi-Key orders and personalised Santa messages.

Who we are and what we collect

Questions? Email vikeyshop@outlook.com.

At checkout, a parent or guardian may provide a child's first name, town, and details to include in a message, such as achievements or gifts. We also collect the order contact and delivery details needed to fulfil the order, such as your name, email address, and delivery address.

How it is used and shared

We use the child's details to create the requested personalised message and set up the Santa Vi-Key. Order details are stored in our online order system and sent to us by email so we can process and fulfil the order.

Payment is handled through NatWest Payit. We do not receive or store your bank login or card details. We share only the information needed with payment and delivery providers to complete your order.

We do not use this information for marketing or advertising, and we do not sell it.

Keeping and deleting Santa details

Personalised messages, pages, links, and other child-specific details are deleted by 31 January each year (31 January 2027 for the 2026 Christmas season). Basic order records that do not include children's details may be kept for as long as required for accounting.

Next Christmas

Your Vi-Key can be reactivated with a new message or a recreation of the old one. Reactivation fees start from £5.99.

Requests about Santa information

You can ask us to see, correct, or delete your or your child's information before the scheduled deletion date. Email vikeyshop@outlook.com.
