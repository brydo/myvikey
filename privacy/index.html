🎄 Our Santa Vi-Key Privacy Promise 🎄

Effective Date: September 2026

🎅 Who We Are

We're the elves behind the Santa Vi-Key. This page explains, in plain words, how we look after the little bits of information you share with us. Questions? Email vikeyshop@outlook.com.

🎁 What We Collect

For your child's message (shared by a parent or guardian at checkout):

  • First name
  • Town or city
  • Any special achievements or gifts you'd like Santa to mention

For your order:

  • Your name, email address, and delivery address

Payments: You pay through NatWest Payit, a secure bank payment service. We never see or store your bank login or card details.

✨ How We Use It

  • Your child's details are used only to create their personalized message from Santa and set up their Vi-Key.
  • Your details are used only to take your order, deliver it, and keep in touch about it.

We never use your information for marketing or advertising, and we never sell it. We only share what's needed with our payment and delivery partners so your order reaches you.

🔒 Keeping It Safe

We only collect what we truly need. Your child's details are used to build their personalized message link, and we don't keep them in any online database.

❄️ Tidying Up After Christmas

Once the festive season ends, we clear everything away. All personalized messages, web pages, and links containing children's details are permanently deleted by 31 January each year (31 January 2027 for this Christmas). Basic order records (which don't include children's details) may be kept for as long as the law requires for accounting.

🎄 Next Christmas

Want the magic again? Your Vi-Key can be reactivated with a brand new message, or a recreation of the old one. Reactivation fees start from £5.99.

💌 Your Rights

Under UK data protection law, you can ask us to see, correct, or delete your or your child's information at any time, including before 31 January. Just email vikeyshop@outlook.com and we'll get back to you promptly. If you're ever unhappy with how we've handled your data, you can contact the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
